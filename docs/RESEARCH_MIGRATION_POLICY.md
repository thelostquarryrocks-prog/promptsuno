# Research Authority and Migration Policy

Status: canonical policy
Effective: 2026-09-27

## Authority rule

Legacy SunoV6.wiki research may be migrated into PromptSuno as research authority, not as automatic product-direction authority.

A legacy source becomes authoritative for PromptSuno only after it is deliberately reviewed and copied, summarized, or linked into the current PromptSuno source set.

## Evidence rules to preserve

PromptSuno research should:

- prioritize official Suno sources
- separate documented features from measured behavior
- preserve version, date, model, and operation context
- treat community evidence carefully
- distinguish observation from speculation
- preserve uncertainty where evidence is weak
- avoid invented deterministic Suno grammar
- avoid guaranteed claims about punctuation, ordering, numeric weighting, tags, section labels, or similar mechanisms without evidence

Research should improve product behavior quietly underneath the interface rather than forcing users to navigate a research database.

## Historical status of SunoV6.wiki

SunoV6.wiki product-direction documents are historical/superseded unless a current PromptSuno document explicitly adopts them.

Historical material may still be valuable for:

- research evidence
- validated UX concepts
- reusable assets
- experiment results
- terminology
- accessibility findings

Do not inherit legacy domain, routing, homepage, implementation, branch/SHA, launch, or backlog decisions by default.

## Current migration inventory

At the baseline commit, the PromptSuno repository contains no migrated legacy research documents and no migrated brand/mascot asset pack.

Therefore no legacy file should be treated as current merely because it existed in the prior project.

When assets or research are migrated, record:

1. source name/version
2. migration date
3. what was retained
4. what was intentionally discarded
5. the PromptSuno destination
6. any evidence/version limitations
